> Purpose: Provide citable guidance for investigation checklists and missing-info prompts.
> Replace with official KYC/CDD policy for production.

## 1. Principle
KYC/CDD aims to establish customer identity and understand risk. Enhanced review may be required when risk level increases or suspicious indicators are triggered.

## 2. Suggested Risk-Based Review Tiers

### 2.1 Standard Due Diligence (SDD)
Applicable when the customer is low-to-medium risk and no strong suspicious indicators are present.

### 2.2 Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
Consider EDD when:
- Risk scoring indicates high risk
- Significant anomalies exist (velocity, amount, location mismatch)
- Links exist to risky clusters or repeated suspicious patterns

## 3. Common Information Requests (Non-PII wording recommended)

### 3.1 Identity & Verification
- Confirmation that identity verification is complete
- Verification status / tier (pass, pending, failed)
- Date of last verification review

### 3.2 Source of Funds / Source of Wealth (as applicable)
- High-level explanation of source of funds
- Supporting documentation as required by policy thresholds

### 3.3 Account Context
- Account age / onboarding date
- Intended account usage (high-level category)
- Expected transaction volume ranges (approximate)

### 3.4 Access & Security Signals (if relevant)
- Device history changes (count / recency)
- IP / region changes (high-level)

## 4. Documentation & Audit Trail
All requests, responses, and investigator judgments should be recorded with timestamps and reason codes for auditability.
